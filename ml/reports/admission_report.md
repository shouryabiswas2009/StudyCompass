# Admission model report (SYNTHETIC data)

> Generated by `ml/train_admission.py`. The data is simulated
> (`ml/generate_synthetic.py`): these numbers show the pipeline works on
> data with a known structure. They are **not** evidence of predicting real
> admissions.

Data: 18300 synthetic applicants across 61 schools,
admit rate 27.3%. Split 10980 train / 3660 validation /
3660 test (stratified). Logistic regression C = 1
(5-fold CV on log-loss). Thresholds chosen for best F1 on the validation set.

## Test-set results

| Model | ROC-AUC ↑ | Log-loss ↓ | Brier ↓ | Threshold | Precision | Recall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline rule | 0.730 | 0.500 | 0.165 | 0.12 | 0.460 | 0.778 |
| Logistic regression | 0.874 | 0.386 | 0.124 | 0.38 | 0.653 | 0.696 |
| Gradient boosting | 0.885 | 0.371 | 0.118 | 0.38 | 0.671 | 0.721 |

Logistic regression beats the baseline rule on all three of ROC-AUC,
log-loss and Brier: **yes**.
